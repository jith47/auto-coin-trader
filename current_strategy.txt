# Improved BTC-DOGE Correlation Scalp Strategy v2.2

## Key Improvements Made:
- **Tightened Relative Strength Filter**: Lowered the overextension threshold from 3x to 2.5x BTC performance to avoid "exhaustion" entries.
- **NYSE Open "Dead Zone"**: Extended the avoid-trading window during NYSE open to 30 minutes to filter out high-frequency bull/bear traps.
- **Delta/CVD Hard Confirmation**: Mandates a specific Delta slope direction to prevent entering during "loading" or stagnant data periods.
- **Aggressive Time-Stop**: Shortened the "no-move" exit window to 10 minutes to preserve capital in sideways markets.

## 3. PRECISE ENTRY TRIGGERS (With Extension Filter)

### Long Scalp Entry Checklist:
```
□ BTC 1D: Price > 21 EMA (confirmed by 2+ daily closes)
□ BTC 4H: Higher low formed within last 8 candles
□ BTC 1H: 
   - Delta sum (10 candles) > +300K
   - CVD making higher highs
□ BTC 1M:
   - Liquidity sweep below recent low (5-15 candles)
   - Immediate reclaim (within 2-3 candles)
   - Volume spike > 2x 20-period SMA
□ DOGE 1M:
   - Correlation > 0.80 (60-period)
   - EXTENSION FILTER: DOGE 1D % Change < (BTC 1D % Change * 2.5) 
     (Example: If BTC is +2%, DOGE must be < +5%. If DOGE is +6%, AVOID LONG)
   - CVD 1M: Must show POSITIVE SLOPE (must not be flat or loading)
   - Enter on first green candle close after BTC reclaim
```

### Short Scalp Entry Checklist:
```
□ BTC 1D: Price < 21 EMA (confirmed by 2+ daily closes)
□ BTC 4H: Lower high formed within last 8 candles  
□ BTC 1H:
   - Delta sum (10 candles) < -300K
   - CVD making lower lows
□ BTC 1M:
   - Liquidity sweep above recent high
   - Immediate rejection (within 2-3 candles)
   - Red volume spike > 2x 20-period SMA
□ DOGE 1M:
   - Correlation > 0.80
   - CVD 1M must be making LOWER LOWS
   - Enter on first red candle close after BTC rejection
```

## 5. EXIT STRATEGY (The "Profit Guard")

### Take Profit (TP):
- **TP1**: 0.3% - 0.4% (Close 50% of position)
- **TP2**: 0.8% - 1.0% (Close remaining 50%)
- **Dynamic TP**: If BTC delta flips from +500K to -100K, exit all immediately.

### Stop Loss (SL):
- **Hard SL**: 0.40% below entry (Tightened to improve R:R)
- **Time SL**: If trade hasn't hit TP1 within 10 minutes, exit at market.
- **Break-even SL**: Move SL to entry once TP1 is hit.
